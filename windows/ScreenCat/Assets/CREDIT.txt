Cat sprites: "Cat Pack" (free tier) by Glem / octopusINKUS
https://octopusinkus.itch.io/cat-pack

Cat_Sprite_Sheet.png and Cat_Shadow.png are the pack's original files, unmodified
on disk. Screen Cat recolours the sheet in memory at load time; the files
themselves are untouched.

Cat_Ball.png is the yarn ball, cropped out of the pack's play animation so the
ball can roll around on its own.

The pack states no licence. It is a name-your-own-price download and is used
here in a personal, non-redistributed desktop toy. If you keep using it, pay the
author and credit them.

This copy is embedded into the Windows exe. Because the pack has no licence,
the repository and any built exe must stay private and must not be shared.
