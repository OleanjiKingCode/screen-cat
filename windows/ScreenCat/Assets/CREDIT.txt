Cat sprites: "Cat Pack" (free tier) by Glem / octopusINKUS
https://octopusinkus.itch.io/cat-pack

Used in Screen Cat with the artist's permission. Cat_Sprite_Sheet.png and
Cat_Shadow.png are the pack's original files, unmodified on disk; Screen Cat
recolours the sheet in memory when it loads. Cat_Ball.png is the yarn ball,
cropped out of the pack's play animation so the ball can roll on its own.
If you want the cat art for something else, get it from the artist's page.

Dog sprites: Dog_Sprite_Sheet.png and Dog_Ball.png are original art made for
Screen Cat (generator: mac/Tools/make_dog.swift).

All of these are embedded in the Windows exe.
